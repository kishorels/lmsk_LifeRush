Privacy Policy — LMSK LifeRush

Package ID: com.lmsk.liferush · Effective Date: November 24, 2026

LMSK LifeRush ("the App") is a 3D endless runner mobile game. We respect your privacy and designed the game to work offline without requiring account creation, login, or personal profile registration.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

LMSK LifeRush does not require an account and does not collect or transmit your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location to any developer server.

The App stores only non-sensitive gameplay preferences locally on your device using browser/WebView storage:

  • Display nickname you optionally choose inside the game (defaults to "Student")
  • Gameplay progress (best distance, coins, XP level, unlocked maps and characters, power upgrades, daily mission progress)
  • Audio, graphics quality, tutorial state, and rewarded ad cooldown timer

This local gameplay data never leaves your device unless you uninstall or clear the App's storage.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

LMSK LifeRush displays Rewarded Video Ads (user-initiated for optional revives, 2x run coins, or bonus coins) and Interstitial Ads (shown between completed runs) using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob).

To deliver, measure, and prevent fraud on advertisements, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device and usage information, including:

  • Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) and device identifiers
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address
  • App interactions, ad views/clicks, and diagnostic/crash performance logs

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at Google Partner Sites Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Opt-Out & Advertising Choices

You can opt-out of personalized ads or reset/delete your Android Advertising ID at any time on your device via Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads), or manage Google ad preferences at Google Ads Settings.

4. Data Deletion

Because LMSK LifeRush does not maintain user accounts or cloud databases, you can permanently delete all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling LMSK LifeRush.

5. Children's Privacy

LMSK LifeRush is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can address it promptly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if features or advertising SDKs change. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or support regarding LMSK LifeRush, please reach out via our support page: https://github.com/kishorels/lmsk_LifeRush/issues.
